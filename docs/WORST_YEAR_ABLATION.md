# Worst-year ablation analysis: C2 and C1 relative to M0

Generated by `scripts/05_eval/ablation_worst_year.py`. The inputs are 45 frozen member-level NPZ files (five members × {M0, C1, C2, C3s1-3, C4adj, C4cf, C4sign}) and the equal-weight five-member `main.npz`. The analysis reads frozen predictions without training or changing thresholds.

`BSS_dev = 1 − BS/BS_clim`, with `BS_clim = π² + (1−2π)·ȳ_year` and π = 0.079102 for 2000–2019. Years come from `row_uid` ISO_TIME. Every NPZ is checked for `split == test`, `protocol == holdout_masked_v1`, and agreement between its file hash and the manifest `output_sha256`. C2 removes the GNN graph pathway; C1 removes all causal losses. **Contrasts in this document are condition − M0, so a negative Δ favours the causal component.**

The analysis asks whether the causal prior reduces the loss in the worst year by more than it changes the pooled score. Both the ensemble and the individual members are evaluated with the same frozen probabilities.

## 8. Interpretation

**Assessment: the hypothesis that the causal prior provides special protection against a poor worst year is not supported on the frozen test set.** The decision rule requires the worst-year contrast to exceed the pooled contrast in magnitude and to have a 90% storm-cluster interval excluding zero, for both the ensemble and the member panel.

**Members.** 2022 is the worst year shared by all five members. Removing the graph pathway lowers BSS_dev in 2/5 members, which favours the prior in that year, and raises it in 3/5. The mean member contrast is -0.008, compared with -0.015 for the pooled years. The worst-year/pooled magnitude ratio is 0.53; a ratio below 1 means that the worst-year loss is not amplified. Fixed-year bootstrap identifies 1 significant members: lora16_sf. Those whose direction supports worst-year protection are lora16_sf. C1 raises BSS_dev in 4/5 members, with mean +0.034, compared with +0.017 for the pooled years. This member pattern does not support a general worst-year protection effect.

**Ensemble.** In 2022, M0 is 0.159, C2 is 0.130 (Δ = -0.029), and C1 is 0.170 (Δ = +0.011). The worst-year and pooled C2 contrasts (-0.032) are of similar magnitude (ratio 0.93), and the 90% CI [-0.089, +0.010] contains zero. The year contributes 0.152 of the pooled C2 contrast, **below its 0.190 row share**. 2020 and 2021 jointly contribute 0.785. The ensemble's graph-pathway gain is therefore smallest in 2022. The pooled C1 contrast is +0.001, so yearly shares are not interpretable at that scale.

**Summary.** The ensemble's graph-pathway benefit is smallest in its worst year, 2022: that year supplies 15% of the pooled loss, below its 19% row share, with ΔC2 = -0.029 and a 90% CI containing zero. The member-level mean worst-year contrast (-0.008) is also smaller in magnitude than the pooled contrast (-0.015). LoRA-sf is the significant exception: Δ = -0.139 in 2022, with 90% CI [-0.301, -0.045]. Its M0 pooled BSS_dev ranks 2/5 (0.209; the highest is temp5_slim at 0.214), so this is a member-specific result rather than a general ensemble effect. The worst year, 2022, combines systematic probability overprediction with the lowest climatological reference score. The data support a calibration diagnosis for that year and a worst-year protection effect only for LoRA-sf.
