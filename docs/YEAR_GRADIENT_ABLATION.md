# Yearly ablation gradient: where the graph pathway helps during 2020–2024

Generated by `scripts/05_eval/ablation_year_gradient.py`. The inputs are 45 member-level frozen NPZ files (five members × {M0, C1, C2, C3s1-3, C4adj, C4cf, C4sign}) and the equal-weight five-member `main.npz`, 46 files in total. This analysis uses M0, C2, and C1. It reads frozen predictions without training or changing thresholds.

**Definition.** `BSS_dev = 1 − BS/BS_clim`, with `BS_clim = π² + (1−2π)·ȳ` and π = 0.079102 for the 2000–2019 development period. **ΔBSS_dev = BSS_dev(M0) − BSS_dev(condition)**, so a positive value favours the causal component. C2 removes the GNN graph branch; C1 removes all causal losses. Years come from `row_uid` ISO_TIME, and the ensemble is the equal-weight mean of the five members.

**Inference.** Paired storm-cluster bootstrap resamples whole `sid` clusters with replacement, B = 2000, seed = 42. The same draw is used for both conditions. Percentile 90% and 95% intervals are reported, with `p_boot = 2·min(P(draw≤0), P(draw≥0))` and lower bound 1/(B+1). `load_frozen`, `bss`, and `DEV_RATE` are shared with `scripts/05_eval/ablation_worst_year.py`. The fixed-year bootstrap uses the opposite contrast sign and adds 95% intervals from the same draws; Section 0 cross-checks it against the shared implementation in four periods.

## 4. Interpretation

1. **The gradient is non-monotonic.** Ensemble ΔC2 is 2020 +0.080, 2021 +0.047, 2022 +0.029, 2023 -0.007, 2024 +0.017; the descending order is 2020 > 2021 > 2022 > 2024 > 2023. The pooled 2020–2021 contrast is +0.063 (90% CI [+0.042, +0.090]), nearly twice the full-test pooled value of +0.032. These two years contribute 44% of the test rows. Each period has its own reference rate, so yearly and pooled contrasts cannot be combined by a simple row-weighted average.

2. **Significance.** The 95% intervals exclude zero in 2020, 2021; the 90% intervals exclude zero in 2020, 2021. The pooled 2020–2021 p_boot is 0.0005.
 2022: the 95% CI [-0.018, +0.107] contains zero (p_boot = 0.2580), and the 90% CI also contains zero.
 2023: the 95% CI [-0.055, +0.044] contains zero (p_boot = 0.8080), and the 90% CI also contains zero.
 2024: the 95% CI [-0.040, +0.059] contains zero (p_boot = 0.4890), and the 90% CI also contains zero.

3. **Member pattern.** Positive counts are 5/5 in 2020 and 4/5 in 2021 (lora16_sf, lora16_temp5, partial4_sf, temp2, temp5_slim are all positive in 2020), then 2/5, 2/5, and 2/5 in 2022–2024. The 2023 ensemble contrast is negative (-0.007), but its interval contains zero. Member intervals are wide because each year contains 19–29 storm clusters, so the member counts describe consistency rather than separate member-level discoveries.

4. **Loss-only control.** The pooled 2020–2021 C1 contrast is +0.0074 (95% CI [-0.0177, +0.0303]). Yearly values are 2020 +0.0153, 2021 -0.0002, 2022 -0.0107, 2023 +0.0075, 2024 -0.0178, and every 95% interval contains zero. The corresponding C2 contrast is +0.063. C1 retains the GNN branch and removes only the three causal losses; its small contrast identifies the graph pathway as the main source of the ensemble gain in this period.

**Summary.** The ensemble graph-removal loss is largest in 2020–2021 (pooled ΔC2 = +0.063, 95% CI [+0.038, +0.098], p_boot = 0.0005), with +0.080 in 2020 and +0.047 in 2021. Positive member counts are 5/5 and 4/5 in those years. The 2022 and 2024 contrasts are positive with intervals containing zero; the 2023 contrast is negative with an interval containing zero. The years with 95% intervals excluding zero are 2020, 2021.
